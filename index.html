Why Killion & Hall

On your side, every step of the way.

Truly Independent

How It Works

Getting covered is simple.

We handle the complexity of the insurance marketplace so you don't have to.

1

Call or stop in

Reach us at (317) 888-1040 or visit our Madison Avenue office. We'll start with a conversation about what you need to protect.

2

We shop the market for you

As an independent agency representing 10+ carriers, we compare rates and coverage options — at no cost to you.

3

Get your personalized recommendation

Our licensed agents walk you through the best options and explain exactly what's covered — in plain English.

4

You're covered — and we stay in touch

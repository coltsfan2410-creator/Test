Summary: Killion & Hall Insurance Agency collects personal information only as needed to provide insurance services, never sells your information to third parties for marketing purposes, and takes your privacy seriously. We are a local Indiana insurance agency, not a data company.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Killion & Hall Insurance Agency for a quote, service, or general inquiry, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, home address, phone number, and email address.
  • Policy and Coverage Information: Details about your property (home, vehicle, business, etc.) needed to provide an accurate insurance quote or manage your policy.
  • Vehicle Information: Year, make, model, VIN, and driver's license numbers for auto insurance purposes.
  • Property Information: Address, square footage, age, and other relevant details for homeowner or business property insurance.
  • Financial Information: General financial information required by insurers to underwrite a policy (e.g., credit-based insurance scores processed by our carrier partners).
  • Claims Information: Details related to any insurance claims you report to us or that are reported on your behalf.
  • Website Usage Data: Anonymous data about how you interact with our website, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

We collect this information directly from you when you call, email, or visit our office, or when you submit information through our website or partner platforms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To obtain insurance quotes from carrier partners on your behalf
  • To bind, issue, and manage your insurance policies
  • To communicate with you about your coverage, renewals, and policy changes
  • To process claims and assist in the claims process
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements applicable to licensed insurance agents in Indiana
  • To send you relevant information about your existing policies or services you have requested
  • To improve our services and website user experience

We do not use your personal information for purposes beyond what is described above without your consent.

3. Sharing of Information

Killion & Hall Insurance Agency does not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Insurance Carriers: We share your information with insurance companies for the purpose of obtaining quotes, binding coverage, or managing claims. These carriers have their own privacy policies.
  • Service Providers: We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating our agency (e.g., agency management software, communication platforms). These parties are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights and safety of our clients, staff, or agency.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of agency assets, personal information may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to the same privacy protections.

Any disclosure of personal information to insurance carriers is necessary to fulfill your request for insurance services and is a standard practice in the insurance industry.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

We may use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the basic functionality of our website.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics). This data helps us improve our website but does not identify you personally.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website. We do not use cookies to track you across third-party websites or sell cookie data.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure communication practices for transmitting sensitive information
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular review of our data collection and storage practices

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have concerns about the security of your information, please contact us directly.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct any inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal or regulatory requirements that require us to retain certain records.
  • Opt-Out of Communications: You may opt out of non-essential communications from us at any time by contacting us or following the unsubscribe instructions in any email we send.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Please note that your ability to access, correct, or delete certain information may be limited by applicable Indiana law and our regulatory obligations as a licensed insurance agency.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete that information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or business operations. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of our services following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
